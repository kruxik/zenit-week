What matters firstEmail and meetings fit around it

There's a classic demonstration: fill a jar with sand first, then try to fit in the big rocks: family, health, growth, meaningful work.

They don't fit.

Reverse it and put the rocks in first, and the sand settles around them naturally.

Most of us plan the week like the first jar. Email, meetings and small tasks fill it up. By Thursday, there's no room left for what really matters.

Who's behind it

Why I built Zenit Week

I'm Petr Burian. For 16 years I was the CTO behind Flashscore, which our teams built for 130 million sports fans. I went through burnout.

Today I mentor founders, CEOs and CTOs. I help them lead with metrics and get real value out of AI.

Near the end of my sabbatical, I worried I wasn't working enough. Then I drew my week as a mind map and it floored me: the Work branch was 10x bigger than anything else. Since then, I plan my week around what matters to me.

Zenit Week is free and open source, so anyone can check that your data stays with you.
