What it solves

        

          Most planners are flat lists — checklists or todos.
        

        

          Rocks first: if you fill the jar with sand, the big rocks won't fit. Most weeks fail the same way — emails, meetings and small tasks crowd out what actually matters.
        

        

          Zenit Week helps you plan visually. Start with your biggest rocks. See balance — or imbalance — at a glance.
        

      


      

        
Key features

        
Visual mindmap — view your week with customizable branches

        
Agenda view — same week, two lenses

        
Stats — balance, trend and completion for the week

        
View levels — Rocks · Pebbles · Sand

        
Day filter — one day at a time, the rest of the week hidden

        
Priorities — Normal · High · Critical (cascade to children)

        
Magic counters — type Nx for a progress counter

        
Magic day selectors — type (mo), (mo, we) or (daily) to schedule

        
Effort baseline — track your weekly effort and overall progress

        
Google Drive sync — your own Drive only (optional)

        
Comments — attach a note to any activity

        
Reusable & transfers — carry unfinished or repeating work into the next week

        
Quick add — drop a task on a branch without touching the map

        
Undo / Redo — made a typo? Revert anytime

        
Export / Import — full backup of every week as one JSON file

        
Works offline — installable, and edits made offline sync when you are back

        
EN / CS, Light / Dark — customize to fit your needs

Magic day indicators

        
Add (day) to any activity name to schedule it for that weekday in the daily list.

        

          
            po út st čt pá so ne
          
        

        

          
            mo tu we th fr sa su
          
        

        
Multiple days: (mo, we, fr) — spaces or commas both work.

        
Range: (mo-we) — expands to Mon, Tue, Wed.

        
Every day: type (daily) and it expands to all 7 days.

        
Relative: (today) or (tomorrow) — frozen to the actual weekday when you save.

        
Right now: (now) — like (today), and pins the item to the top of today's agenda.

      


      

        
Magic counters

        
Add Nx to an activity name (e.g. Do Pushups 3x) to create a magic counter.

      


      

        
Activity rebinding & organizing

        
Drag a node over another to rebind it as a child. Drag between siblings to reorder.

      


      

        
Canvas Navigation

        
Drag background to pan. Scroll / pinch to zoom.

        
Click the logo to fit everything to the view.

        
Arrow keys move a focus ring from node to node — every activity hotkey then works on the focused node.
